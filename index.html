A Little More About Myself.

As a high school student at Shenzhen Nanshan Foreign Language Senior High School, I am passionate about Computer Science and leveraging technology to solve real-world problems. My academic journey has been driven by curiosity, persistence, and a commitment to truth.

Beyond academics, I co-led the IELTS Club as Co-President and taught its Listening section to expand access to learning resources, contributed to and helped maintain a widely used Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science revision guide, and served as a student ambassador in international exchange programs. I believe leadership is not about authority — it is about lifting others up and making yourself replaceable.

I enjoy working out, listening to music, and exploring the latest technology. My goal is to study Computer Science and contribute my own piece to the vast universe of knowledge.

Contributor

Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science Revision Note

2-year project

Unable to find a resource that explained Cambridge International AS & A Level Computer Science clearly enough, I reached out to the senior author of an existing guide and spent two years improving and maintaining it through corrections, explanations, and new material. Alongside this work, I collected Computer Science exam-preparation resources, uploaded them to Google Drive, incorporated the files into the revision note, and shared the complete collection free of charge through social media. I then trained a junior student to help maintain the guide, supporting its continued use after I moved on.

"With commitment to truth, even a small individual action can make a huge difference to a global community — and true leadership means making myself replaceable."

Organizer & Host

Cross-School Academic Experience Sharing Seminar

6 Senior Students from 6 High Schools

🎙️

I noticed that junior high school students in my community lacked access to firsthand advice on how to navigate the transition to senior high school and prepare for the senior high school entrance examination (Zhongkao). Instead of waiting for someone else to bridge that gap, I took the initiative — I contacted my former junior high homeroom teacher to understand students' needs and help coordinate the seminar, reached out across my network, personally invited six senior students from six different high schools, designed the agenda, coordinated schedules, and hosted the entire seminar myself.

"Leadership is not about having a title or waiting for permission — it is about seeing a need, bringing the right people together, and creating a space where others can grow."

Campus Volunteer

School 30th Anniversary

Shenzhen Nanshan Foreign Language Senior High School

I served as a campus volunteer during my school's 30th anniversary celebration, welcoming returning alumni, guiding visitors, and helping the event run smoothly. It was not a leadership role with a title — no one reported to me, no crisis demanded my intervention. But standing there, watching alumni from different generations share laughter and memories, I understood something profound: a school is not just a building, it is a living community woven together by everyone who has ever passed through its halls.

Giving back that day did not feel like work. It felt like a quiet thank-you to the place that shaped me. I realised that the truest form of leadership does not always come with a badge — sometimes it is simply showing up, holding the door, and making someone feel welcome. In a world that celebrates the loud and the visible, there is power in the quiet, steady act of service.

"Service is leadership in its quietest form. Not every contribution needs applause — some of the most meaningful ones happen in the background, unseen but deeply felt."
