A Little More About Myself.

As a high school student at Shenzhen Nanshan Foreign Language School, I am passionate about Computer Science and leveraging technology to solve real-world problems. My academic journey has been driven by curiosity, persistence, and a commitment to truth.

Beyond academics, I founded the IELTS Club to ensure equal access to learning resources, co-authored a widely-used Cambridge CS revision guide, and served as a student ambassador in international exchange programs. I believe leadership is not about authority — it is about lifting others up and making yourself replaceable.

I enjoy working out, listening to music, and exploring the latest in machine learning. My goal is to study Computer Science and contribute my own piece to the vast universe of knowledge.

Cambridge AS & A Level CS Revision Note

Organizer & Host

Cross-School Academic Experience Sharing Seminar

6 Senior Students from 6 High Schools

I noticed that junior high school students in my community lacked access to firsthand advice on how to navigate the transition to senior high school and prepare for international exams. Instead of waiting for someone else to bridge that gap, I took the initiative — I reached out across my network, personally invited six senior students from six different high schools, designed the agenda, coordinated schedules, and hosted the entire seminar myself.

"Leadership is not about having a title or waiting for permission — it is about seeing a need, bringing the right people together, and creating a space where others can grow."
